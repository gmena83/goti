## How to use Base44 in 10 steps

### 01. Start with a clear prompt

The foundation of any great app begins with how you communicate your vision to Base44. The platform's AI can understand natural language, but **specificity makes all the difference**. Using a prompt guide can help you structure your ideas clearly.

When crafting your initial prompt, include the core functionality, who will use it, and what problem it solves. This context helps Base44 make better decisions about database structure, user flows, and interface design.

**Example:** Instead of saying "Build me a CRM," try: *"Build a CRM for a small marketing agency with contacts, deal tracking, and a dashboard showing revenue by client and project stage."*

### 02. Use app templates

Starting from scratch can feel overwhelming. Base44's template library offers a smarter approach: find something close to your vision and customize it. Browse through the available templates and look for apps that share similar workflows or user interactions. You can clone a template (e.g., a project management template) and ask Base44 to modify it with event-specific features like RSVP tracking and venue management. This approach significantly cuts your development time by building on proven foundations.

### 03. Master the visual editor

Base44's visual editor gives you pixel-level control without requiring design expertise. You can adjust spacing, colors, fonts, and layouts with a few simple clicks. The system automatically maintains responsive design. Don't be afraid to experiment; Base44's version control means you can always roll back changes if something doesn't work.

### 04. Keep your app clean with states and conditions

States and conditions transform a static webpage into a dynamic, interactive application. They control what users see based on their actions, permissions, or data. They are the logic that makes your app respond intelligently.

Start with simple states (e.g., showing different content for logged-in versus anonymous users) and expand to more complex scenarios (e.g., displaying admin panels only for administrators). If you're unsure how to implement a particular state, use the AI chat to describe the behavior you want.

### 05. Use integrations to supercharge your app

Base44's integration capabilities transform your app into a connected part of your workflow. The platform includes built-in connections to popular services like Gmail, Google Calendar, Stripe, and Slack. Start with a simple integration, like a Slack notification on form submission, to understand the system. As you become comfortable, explore more complex workflows, such as connecting to your existing CRM or syncing data with accounting software.

### 06. Preview in full-screen mode while you work

The full-screen preview mode is a crucial tool for spotting design issues and testing user flows. Many problems only become apparent when you view your app the way your users will. Make a full-screen preview a regular part of your development process to catch usability problems early.

### 07. Run a security scan to catch what you miss

Security is critical. Base44's **Security scan** feature automatically checks your app for common vulnerabilities, like exposed API keys, unsafe data handling, or broken permission structures. Make security scanning part of your regular development routine to save you from serious problems down the road.

### 08. Use workspace visibility for internal tools

Not every app needs to be public from day one. Base44's workspace visibility settings allow you to control who can access your app at different stages of development. This is valuable for internal tools, staging environments, or client review processes. Set apps to "Workspace" visibility when building tools for your team, then switch to public when you're ready to launch.

### 09. Ask questions in the AI chat

Base44's AI chat mode is designed to be your collaborative partner. Learning to ask better questions can dramatically improve your results. Treat AI like an experienced developer who can explain complex concepts. When something isn't working, ask for an explanation, not just a fix. Use **Discuss Mode** for brainstorming and planning without affecting your live app.

### 10. Keep iterating, fast

Base44's greatest strength is enabling **rapid iteration**. You don't need to get everything perfect on the first try. Build something functional quickly and improve it based on real usage and feedback. Release early versions to small groups of users and gather their feedback to guide your next iteration. Iteration is not just about fixing problems; it's about discovering new opportunities.
