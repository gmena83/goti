## 1. What is Vibe Coding? (00:31)

Vibe coding is a new landscape of tools where you can use **prompt and natural language** to describe a feature or a product, and the AI will go off and build that product. The code is completely abstracted from you.

Traditionally, design is pretty hard in these tools. They're slowly but surely adding design features, but really these revolve around simple things like paddings, margins, and border radius. The intent is to let the AI design it for you, which in of itself is derivative and based off of previous designs. You're never going to get anything truly creative, but you are going to get something functional.

**Vibe Code apps are not aimed at designers.** They're aimed at developers, product managers, CEOs, and people who want to build a prototype, get something working, and build a business around an idea that they've had.

**Definition of Vibe Coding:** A prompt-first interface primarily focused on prototypes, developers, product managers, and CEOs—people with ideas. Design control is minimal, even when providing the design.

## 2. When Should You Vibe Code? (02:25)

You should choose Vibe Coding when:
*   The product is something you want to get out quickly and have users use it.
*   You can get away with a basic design (design only prototype or idea first).
*   You are testing the idea, whether you can get users, and what the UX/onboarding journeys are like.
*   You are building **SaaS (Software as a Service)** products that only exist online.
*   You don't want to think about the infrastructure (hosting, databases, authentication).
*   You are prototyping or playing around with an idea.

## 3. When Should You No Code? (02:55)

You should choose No Code when:
*   **Design is paramount.**
*   You need a strong design focus, as no-code tools have a user base of designers who are in their element creating exactly what they want in tools like Webflow, Framer, and Figma.
*   You are building **marketing websites** (agency websites, mostly static content, with editable areas like a blog).
*   You need a high level of design control.

**Vibe coding really isn't for production-ready applications or at least ones that are meant to really scale.**

## 5. Conclusion

The future of coding is a spectrum. Vibe coding is for rapid prototyping and idea validation, abstracting away the code and infrastructure. No-code is for design-focused applications, particularly marketing websites. The key is to choose the right tool for the right job.
